## A place for slower thoughts

I started this blog to give my observations somewhere to live. A photograph, a paragraph, or a note from a journey can become a small record worth returning to.

![A canoe on a still mountain lake](/showcase/slide-1.jpg)

## What I want to share

- Notes from walks and travels.
- Ideas about design and typography.
- Projects made with curiosity and care.

Writing here is a way to **pay attention** and to make a personal archive, one entry at a time.

> A small story written today can be a useful memory tomorrow.

### A simple publishing routine

1. Write a draft in Markdown.
2. Add photographs and descriptive alternative text.
3. Commit the file and let the website build.

You can explore the [project archive](/projects/) or return to [all posts](/posts/).
