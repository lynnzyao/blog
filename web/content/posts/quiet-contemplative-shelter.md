## Framing the landscape

A thoughtful shelter makes room for both people and the world outside. Deep overhangs soften the light; a simple window turns a mountain view into a daily observation.

![An alpine lake beneath the mountains](/showcase/slide-2.jpg)

## Materials and attention

Timber, stone, and a quiet place to sit can be enough. The most interesting spaces invite us to notice the changing weather rather than distract us from it.
