## Before the season turns

The ridge catches the morning light before the valley does. Walking above the treeline, the landscape becomes a study of granite, shadow, and distance.

![Mountain peaks in the first light of sunrise](/showcase/slide-3.jpg)

## Notes from the trail

- Start early enough to watch the light change.
- Pause to record details rather than only the view.
- Leave room in the journal for what the photographs miss.

These are the small observations I want to keep from a journey between seasons.
